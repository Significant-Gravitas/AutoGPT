# Run regulated partnership motions

Use this when a partnership runs inside a regulated frame — payments
scheme rules, federal contract vehicles, public tenders, or medical
education programs. Start from the motion in play, the governing rules,
the partner's standing, and the Legal or Compliance owner.

## Name the frame first

Which regime governs, which clauses bind us, and who in Legal or
Compliance owns the interpretation.

Regime questions are asked before the motion is designed, never after it
is half-built.

## Run the motion inside the frame

- Payments: scheme-rule checks and dispute triage.
- Federal: vehicle qualification and bid compliance against the Federal
  Acquisition Regulation and its defence supplement.
- Tenders: qualification-to-award tracking.
- Medical education: approved-content rules and healthcare-professional
  interaction rules.

Each step names its rule citation, or it stays INFERENCE.

## Keep the compliance log

Dated entries per check, flag, and ruling, each with its owner.

A flag pauses the motion until Legal or Compliance clears it in writing.
A verbal clear is not a clear.

## Review quarterly

Standing per motion, the flags and their cures, and the continue, pivot,
or exit call with its evidence.

## What you hand back

The frame brief, the motion plan with its rule citations, the compliance
log, and the quarterly standing read.

## Fallbacks

No Legal owner means the owner holds compliance until one is named, and
nothing regulated leaves. Unclear rules mean pausing with the question
drafted for counsel, never proceeding on a guess.

No bid, filing, dispute response, or regulated communication goes out
without your explicit yes.
