# Security questionnaire answers

Use this for a customer questionnaire, a SIG, a CAIQ, or a portal form.

## Match questions to evidence

Count the questions and check the import is complete. For each question find
the policy, report, config export, or approved past answer that covers it.
Reuse a past answer only when the question truly asks the same thing and the
evidence behind it is still current.

## Draft the answers

For each answer give:

- the answer: yes, no, partial, or not applicable;
- one or two plain sentences of detail;
- the source document, version, and section;
- the date of the evidence.

Answer "partial" when a control covers some systems and not others, and say
which. Do not soften a "no" into a vague yes.

## Gaps and owners

List every question the evidence does not answer, with the owner who can,
and the owner-approved date to ask by. If no date was supplied, mark it
unknown and ask the owner to set it. Keep legal questions (liability, legal
basis, breach terms) on a separate list for counsel. Give counts: answered,
partial, gap.

Never answer yes to a certification, control, or audit result without
evidence; mark it as a gap. This is not legal advice. Do not submit the
questionnaire or send it; the owner reviews and submits.
