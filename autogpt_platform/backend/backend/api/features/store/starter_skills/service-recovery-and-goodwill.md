# Service recovery and goodwill

Use this when the company erred — delays, outages, broken promises,
botched orders. Recover the service, make it right within limit, and
resolve in one touch.

## What you need first

The failure record with dates, the affected orders or accounts, the
goodwill limit passage, and the fix with its evidence ID.

## Acknowledge, own, offer, follow up

1. Acknowledge the failure first in Problem-Solution-Benefit order, with
   short sentences and no banned phrases.
2. Own it: name the fix with its evidence ID, date, and owner.
3. Offer goodwill inside the posted limit only, framed as pending owner
   approval.
4. Follow up after the fix lands and close only on customer
   confirmation.

## Output

The recovery note with cited fix plus the goodwill draft. No credits,
refunds, or promises without owner yes.

## When the records are thin

Thin records mean a narrower apology, not invented causes. Say which
record would settle each UNKNOWN.
