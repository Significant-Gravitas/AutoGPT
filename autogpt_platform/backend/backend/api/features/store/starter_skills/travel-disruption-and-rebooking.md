# Travel disruption and rebooking

Use this when trips break — canceled or delayed flights, missed
connections, hotel overbooks — or at check-in, check-out, and the
front desk. Rebook fast and keep the traveler moving.

## What you need first

The booking record (the PNR booking reference, flights, hotel), the
disruption notice with times, traveler location and documents, and the
rebook or refund policy.

## Locate, option, rebook, brief

1. Locate the traveler and the break: what canceled or slipped, and
   what still holds.
2. Option the recovery: fastest rebook, backup routing, hotel or meal
   coverage per policy.
3. Rebook or hold per policy, framing money moves as pending approval,
   and confirm the new itinerary against the record.
4. Brief the traveler with next step, times, and owner, plus the
   front-desk or gate ask.

## Output

The rebooked itinerary with confirmations plus the traveler brief
draft. No charges or promises without owner yes.

## When the records are thin

Thin records mean fewer confirmed legs, not guessed flights. Say which
record would settle each UNKNOWN.
