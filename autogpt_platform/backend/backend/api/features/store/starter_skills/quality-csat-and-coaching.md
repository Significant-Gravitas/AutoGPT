# Quality CSAT and coaching

Use this when the team needs a report card, not a feeling. Score real
threads, track CSAT and average handle time (AHT) from the records,
and draft the one fix per rep.

## What you need first

The thread sample with dates, CSAT and AHT records with source and
period, and the QA scorecard or talk track in play.

## Sample, score, trend, coach

1. Sample real threads per rep per week at the agreed size, quoting the
   scored lines.
2. Score each thread against the talk track: ack speed, next step with
   date and owner, positive language, one question at a time.
3. Trend CSAT and AHT from the records with source and period, never
   from memory.
4. Draft one fix per rep with the quoted line, the better line, and a
   re-check date.

## Output

The scored sample with trends plus per-rep coaching drafts. Offer to
save the fixes as macros or knowledge-base (KB) entries.

## When the records are thin

Thin records mean fewer scored threads, not padded scores. Say which
record would settle each UNKNOWN.
