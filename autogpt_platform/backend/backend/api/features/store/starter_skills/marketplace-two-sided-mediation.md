# Marketplace two-sided mediation

Use this when buyer and seller (or guest and host, traveler and
property) both have a case. Hear both sides, mediate plainly, and
justify any exception.

## What you need first

Both sides' records (orders, messages, photos), the marketplace policy
passage for the dispute type, and payment and fee history.

## Hear, weigh, mediate, draft

1. Hear both sides from the records: what each claims, what the evidence
   confirms, and what stays UNKNOWN.
2. Weigh the claims against the policy passage, never inventing a rule
   the docs do not state.
3. Mediate plainly: name the fair outcome, justify any exception from
   the cited passage, and keep money inside approval limits.
4. Draft a message per side with next step, date, and owner, staged for
   owner yes.

## Output

The mediation decision with cited policy plus per-side drafts. No
payouts, waivers, or exceptions without owner yes.

## When the records are thin

Thin records mean a narrower ruling, not invented facts. Say which
record would settle each UNKNOWN.
