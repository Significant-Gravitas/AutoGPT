# Customer success plan

Use this for a shared account plan or an internal working draft.

## Anchor the plan

Record the customer's outcomes in their words, why each matters, baseline,
measure, target, source, executive sponsor, working owner, and review date.
Mark a proposed target as proposed until the customer agrees.

## Add the work

For each outcome list milestones, owner on each side, due date, dependencies,
completion evidence, and current status. Add open product, support, training,
data, and change-management risks only when they apply. Link every action to an
outcome; remove routine activity with no clear purpose.

## Keep it current

At each review, show what changed, evidence received, decisions made, blocked
items, and the next owner-led action. Keep an assumption and decision log.

Never invent agreement, progress, customer sentiment, or a target. Do not turn
an internal plan into a customer commitment, and do not send it without owner
approval.
