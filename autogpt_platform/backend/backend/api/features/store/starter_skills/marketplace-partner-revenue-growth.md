# Marketplace partner revenue growth

Run this when growing a travel or marketplace partner's revenue
through their listing, rates, promos, and partner-tool reads.

## Inputs

The partner property or listing, current content, rate, and
availability state, the promo and member-deal calendar, and the
competitive and partner reports you can read.

## Audit the listing as a booker sees it

Content completeness, rate logic across dates, and availability gaps.
Score what is missing before you touch what exists.

## Fix content, rates, and availability first

Complete the content in the partner tools, align rates to demand and the
competitive set, and close bogus unavailability. Each change is dated so the
revenue read can trace cause to effect.

## Layer promos with intent

Dynamic promos for soft dates, member deals for loyalty demand,
mobile offers for the mobile mix. Every promo has a window, a target,
and a read date.

## Read the reports weekly

Rate position, visibility, and conversion against the competitive set. A
rank drop gets a dated diagnosis, not a shrug.

## Advise in revenue terms

What to change, what it should do to bookings, and when you will
read it together. Advice is one page with three actions max.

## Log the loop

Change, date, expected effect, actual read. What worked becomes the
playbook for the next property.

## Output

Listing audit with dated fixes, promo calendar with read dates,
weekly competitive-set read, one-page partner advice, and a change-to-effect
log.

## Fallbacks

No report access: work from the public listing labeled FACT and ask
for the partner-tool invite. No booking data: track visibility and
rate-position proxies until revenue data lands.

## Approval gate

Nothing goes to the partner and no rate or promo is changed without
your explicit yes.
