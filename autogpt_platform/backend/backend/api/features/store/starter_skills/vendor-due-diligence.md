# Vendor due diligence

This is an evidence and routing check, not a certification.

## Build the checklist from the purchase

Use the data, access, geography, service criticality, spend, and policy thresholds in the requirements brief. Assign each check to its real owner:

- security controls and test reports to security;
- personal-data terms and locations to privacy;
- insurance to the policy owner;
- financial evidence to finance or procurement;
- contract terms to counsel;
- service history and references to the business owner.

## Record evidence

For each item, keep the vendor claim, document name, scope, issuer, date, expiry, relevant passage, reviewer, status, and open question. Use `PRESENT`, `MISSING`, `EXPIRED`, `OUT OF SCOPE`, or `REVIEWER DECISION`.

Do not call a vendor secure, compliant, solvent, safe, approved, or low risk. Do not accept a badge or summary as proof beyond its stated scope.

## Output

Return the evidence register, missing items, conflicts, and a reviewer queue with due dates. Only the named reviewers decide whether evidence clears their gate.
