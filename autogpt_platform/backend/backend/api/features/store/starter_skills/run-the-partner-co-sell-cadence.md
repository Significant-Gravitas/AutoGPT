# Run the partner co-sell cadence

Use this for the weekly operating rhythm with an active partner. Start
from the partner and the joint plays, CRM movement since the last call,
the deal registration log, and the open actions from last time.

## Prep the call, most-stuck-first

What moved, what stalled, and one action per side per stuck deal. When
several partners share one prep, take the deepest pipeline first.

Layer the rhythm underneath: weekly updates, bi-weekly joint opportunity
reviews, quarterly business planning. Score partner fit per opportunity
before co-selling anything, and name the sellers on our side who actually
evangelise the partner — then propose quota relief or accelerators on
co-sold deals to the owner rather than assuming the partner side is
motivated on its own.

## Keep deal registration honest

The eligibility bar comes first: net-new, BANT-qualified — budget,
authority, need, timeline — and the partner in good standing.

Track every registration through Submitted, In-review, Approved, or
Action required with the reviewer's comments attached. Decide inside a
48-hour SLA, with a reminder at 24 hours and an escalation at 48. Protect
for 60 days, alert 7 days before expiry, and allow exactly one 30-day
renewal with sign-off. Never auto-renew.

Every claimed deal names its registration identifier and date, its state,
its window, and its conflict check. Closes need a Closed-Lost reason.

Pre-write the edge rulings so nobody improvises them live: multi-partner
splits, the customer picking a different partner, and reverse
registrations — the qualified lead shells we hand to partners.

Flag duplicates, expired windows, and registrations with no activity
behind them. Abuse gets named, not smoothed over.

## Rescue stuck joint deals with exactly one motion

Diagnose in order: champion gone, buyer cold, competitor in, paper
process, partner deprioritised. Then name the one move, its owner, its
date, and what happens if it fails.

Anything that needs direct-sales closing goes to the account executive
with a clean brief. You never close it yourself.

Run a quarterly prune alongside the rescues: cut partners with no
strategic match and no delivery, and file the quarterly conflict report
with its count and its first-to-register sustained rate.

## Close with one partner ask

The single thing we need from them this week. One ask, not a list.

## What you hand back

The call prep (moved, stalled, actions), the registration hygiene list,
the rescue lines, and the one partner ask.

## Fallbacks

No CRM movement to read means you prep from the last call notes and the
open actions, and say the data is stale rather than inventing progress.
Nothing partner-facing leaves without your yes.
