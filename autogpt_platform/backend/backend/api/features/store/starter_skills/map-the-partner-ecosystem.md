# Map the partner ecosystem

Use this when the question is category-level rather than partner-level.
Start from the category, the current partners, CRM account overlaps, and
any technology-stack or market signals on hand.

## Draw the map by category

Who plays in each category, split by motion — solutions and services
against independent software vendors (ISVs) and technology partners — our
coverage per category, and the evidence behind every placement.

A category with no evidence behind it is UNKNOWN, not empty.

## Score the whitespace

Each gap gets potential, strategic fit, and reachability, with one
evidence line each. Add integration-attached retention as a value signal:
accounts with an integration live retain better, and that is worth
naming.

Rank the gaps. Cut the ones with no path — a gap we cannot reach is not
an opportunity.

## Call build, buy, or partner

One paragraph of rationale per gap. A partner gap gets a candidate
thesis: the partner type, the value exchange, and the first three
prospect names where the evidence supports them.

## Card the shortlist

One card per opportunity — potential, impact, fit — and hand the
qualified names to the sourcing motion with the thesis attached, rather
than as a bare list.

## What you hand back

The category map, the ranked whitespace list, the build/buy/partner
calls, and the opportunity cards.

## Fallbacks

Thin CRM data means a shorter map built from public research, marked
INFERENCE, with the data that would grow it named explicitly.
