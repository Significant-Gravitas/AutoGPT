# Knowledge centered service

Use this when an answer should outlive the case. Capture knowledge in the
flow of solving, keep articles findable and right, and coach reuse.

## What you need

The solved case with its evidence, the knowledge-base (KB) home and article
template from prefs, and the search terms the customer used.

## Run the loops

1. Run the Solve Loop on every case: search early and search often with the
   customer's own words, use what you find or flag the gap, solve the case,
   then capture what was missing. Searching is creating: preserve the search
   phrases as the seed of the new article. Knowledge capture is part of the
   solve, not after-work.
2. Draft the article from the template: problem in the customer's words,
   environment or eligibility, steps verified against the case record, and
   the fix with its receipt — complete thoughts, not complete sentences. When
   improving an article, add to the issue statements; never rewrite, edit, or
   delete existing ones. If confident and licensed, fix it; otherwise flag it.
   Every step carries its source; a step you cannot verify is cut, never
   guessed. Never publish before the owner says yes.
3. Score article quality on reuse, not prose: findability (would the
   customer's words hit it), accuracy (steps match the record), and
   completeness (edge cases named). Reuse is review: link each article to the
   cases it solved. Retire or merge dupes; a stale article past review gets
   flagged to the KB owner, not silently edited.
4. Track the Solve Loop weekly: reuse rate, self-serve resolution, failed
   searches, and unresolved bot turns. The top failed search becomes the next
   article draft; the top reused article gets a freshness check.
5. Coach the team on capture: one quoted miss per rep per week (solved
   without searching, solved without capturing), with the fixed habit named.
   Log captures, flags, and publishes.
6. Run the Evolve Loop monthly with the KB owner: content-health review
   (a scored article sample, archive or merge, domain gaps), the New-vs-Known
   check, and root-cause feedback to product. The weekly loop keeps answers
   flowing; the monthly loop keeps the collection healthy.

## Output

The staged article or fix, the quality score with evidence, the weekly Solve
metrics and monthly Evolve review, and the coach note.

## Fallbacks

No KB access means staged drafts in chat with the publish path named, not
filed guesses. Never invent article URLs or review dates.
