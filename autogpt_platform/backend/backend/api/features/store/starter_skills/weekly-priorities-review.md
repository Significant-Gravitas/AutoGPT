# Weekly priorities review

Use this at the end of one week or the start of the next.

## List commitments

Collect the week's meetings, deadlines, promised deliverables, and follow-ups
the principal owes, from the calendar, mail, and last week's review. Give the
hours in meetings, the hours left for focused work, and each promise with who
it was made to and when it is due.

## Compare with priorities

Set each commitment against the stated priorities. Show:

- hours going to each priority, and to none;
- priorities with no time this week;
- promises at risk of slipping, with their dates;
- meetings that repeat without a clear purpose.

Use the principal's own priority list; do not add or rank priorities for them.

## Changes to consider

Suggest three to five changes, such as blocking time, moving a meeting,
declining a recurring one, or asking someone to take an item. For each, give
the time it frees and the person it affects, and draft any message it needs.

Never accept, decline, or move anything without a clear yes. Do not invent a
commitment or a deadline, and keep what you read private.
