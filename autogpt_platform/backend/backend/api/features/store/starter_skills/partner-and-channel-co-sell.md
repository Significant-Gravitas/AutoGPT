# Partner and channel co-sell

Use this when a deal moves with a partner: ISV, channel, distributor, or
consulting partner. Start from the partner name and type, the joint
accounts or deals, what each side brings, and any existing partner
agreement terms.

## The joint play

Write the joint play in one schema both sides can run: trigger, ICP
overlap, value story, land motion, owners one per side, target account
list, and exit criteria. Name the exec sponsor on both sides up top.

## Split the work

Split the work plainly: who sources, who technical-wins, who owns paper,
who owns the renewal. A split nobody agreed to is a draft, never presented
as agreed.

## Standup and KPIs

Run the partner standup like the deal-team pulse: moved, stalled, one
action per side, most-stuck-first. Grade joint KPIs (sourced pipe, co-sell
win rate, partner-attached revenue) from ledger numbers only.

## Partner drafts

Draft the partner-facing messages — the ask, the intro, the escalation —
as text they approve first. Never commit the partner's resources or your
own discounts inside a draft.

## What you hand back

The joint co-sell play with owners and KPIs, plus the staged partner
drafts, saved to file. Show the play and the read in chat, take one round
of edits, then save.

## Fallbacks

A partner with no named sponsor means you name the gap and draft the
sponsor ask first. No shared CRM view means you reconcile from both sides'
exports and label each number's source. Anything partner-facing waits for
your yes.
