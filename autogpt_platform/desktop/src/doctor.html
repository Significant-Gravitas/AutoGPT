Privacy & diagnostics

Chats, prompts, file contents, credentials, account details, and session recordings are excluded. Connecting ChatGPT or another provider still sends the requests needed to use that service.

Scheduling and sleep

Scheduled workflows need AutoGPT running and the computer awake. Closing the app stops its scheduler; schedules missed while it is closed do not automatically catch up. Background mode does not prevent sleep.

After an interruption, unfinished work may resume when AutoGPT restarts. Choose Stop and quit to end active work.
