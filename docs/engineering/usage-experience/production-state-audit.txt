Cross-cutting cautions
---------------------
- Production G chooses daily reset first even when weekly is the only exhausted window.
  New later blocking reset is a correctness improvement, not just copy.
- Comparison dates and account/payment details are fixtures. Do not represent them as
  real user or real payment data. Same dates/currency/plan pricing should align across panels.
- Payment-method last4 and invoice quote in new design need actual billing data; current
  PM does not show these. New exact charge is a sample, not guaranteed $50 tax-free charge.
- Current paid plans support annual billing and scheduled changes. A monthly sample
  redesign must retain those supported controls in implementation.
- No new trials should mention Max. Existing prod paywall can show multiple plans;
  distinguish active trial upgrade offer from ineligible NO_TIER plan selection.
- Global shell and unchanged automation-credit components are context, not claimed redesign.
- No browser resources were created by this read-only audit.
