# A new sidebar, a smarter composer, and Otto on Telegram

*July 15 – July 22, 2026*

**Platform versions:** `v0.6.67` · `v0.6.68` · `v0.6.69`

## Navigation moves to a sidebar

The top navigation bar is gone. Every page now hangs off a sidebar on the left, with a new task button and search at the top, the main pages in the middle and your recent chats grouped underneath. Collapse it to a rail of icons when you want the room. Your profile menu was rebuilt at the same time: Profile, Settings, Billing and Activity sit in one group, What's new and Help in another, and Log out at the bottom. [#13628](https://github.com/Significant-Gravitas/AutoGPT/pull/13628)

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/v0667-sidebar-hero.gif" alt="The AutoGPT app with the new left sidebar open, showing the main pages and recent chats"><figcaption><p>The sidebar replaces the top navigation bar across the whole app</p></figcaption></figure>

## Everything you add to a chat starts at the plus button

The composer has one menu for context and setup. Press **+** to attach a file, open your Integrations, Skills or Scheduled tasks without leaving the chat, or start a guided flow: **New skill** and **New scheduled task** pre-fill the composer with a prompt that tells Otto what you want, so you describe it and Otto sets it up. [#13489](https://github.com/Significant-Gravitas/AutoGPT/pull/13489)

## Chat with Otto on Telegram

Telegram joins Discord on the Bots page in Settings. Message the bot directly or @mention it in a group, send files and photos both ways, and in a direct message watch the reply type itself out live. Otto can also post on its own: ask for a Monday update in a channel or a daily note in your DMs and it lands there on schedule. [#13561](https://github.com/Significant-Gravitas/AutoGPT/pull/13561) [#13565](https://github.com/Significant-Gravitas/AutoGPT/pull/13565) [#13588](https://github.com/Significant-Gravitas/AutoGPT/pull/13588) [#13597](https://github.com/Significant-Gravitas/AutoGPT/pull/13597)
