# Meet your Experts: hire a specialist and put it to work

*September 19, 2026*

**Platform version:** `v0.8.0`

This release brings Experts to everyone. An Expert is an AI teammate with a job title, a personality, its own skills and workflows, its own memory and its own computer. You hire one from the Marketplace or raise one yourself, and from day one it works for you: on a schedule, in its own chat thread, inside the limits you set. Otto stays your head of AI and manages the team.

Much of this was built over the summer behind a flag; the pull requests below link to where each piece landed.

## Hire an Expert, or raise your own

The Marketplace opens with a roster of Experts across Marketing, Sales, Finance, Support, Operations, Research, Content and Development. Each card says what the Expert does, which services it needs, what it costs to run, and which skills and workflows come with it. Hire one and it installs its skills and workflows, asks for the connections it needs, and starts its first day of work. If none fits, raise your own: pick a name, a job title and a face, write the Soul document that shapes how it thinks and writes, and hand it the skills and workflows it should own. New accounts get the same choice during onboarding, with recommendations drawn from what you said about your work. ## Every Expert has a page you can share

Each Expert has its own page at a real URL, so you can send it to a colleague or find it from a search engine. The page shows the Expert's role and category, what it sets up on day one, the skills it comes with, the workflows it runs and how often, the services it needs, and an honest note that it is an AI. Signed-out visitors see the full profile and a Get started button that brings them back to the Expert after sign-up. ## Give your team standing work

Routines are the work an Expert does without being asked: a Monday pipeline review, a daily inbox sweep, a one-off report next Thursday. An Expert arrives with its routines switched off; turn one on and it becomes a real schedule, runs in the Expert's own thread, and stays quiet when there is nothing new. Otto can set up routines for itself too, and it manages every schedule on the team. Each Expert only runs the workflows you installed on it, with the connections you granted it. ## A Marketplace for skills

Skills are playbooks an Expert or Otto reads before doing a job: a brand voice guide, a cohort analysis method, an outreach sequence. The Marketplace now has a Skills shelf and a browse page with hundreds of them. Install one onto Otto or straight onto one of your Experts, upload your own as a package, and publish it for others.
